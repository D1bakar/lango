Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

No. Lango is frontend-only — your streak, XP, and word mastery live in your browser's local storage.

Does it work offline?

Yes. A service worker caches the app and courses, so lessons keep working without a connection.

How long is a lesson?

About five minutes: five flashcards and a five-question check. Ten XP per correct answer.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Export your data from Settings, then import the JSON on the other device.
